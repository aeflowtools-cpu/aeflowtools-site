Questions

What's free and what costs money?

The extension and the Claude skill are both free to download, and you get 3 free builds on every computer. After that CodeFlow needs a licence key: $20, one time, for everyone.

What happens after the 3 free builds?

CodeFlow asks for a licence key before it builds more. Everything you've already built stays in your After Effects project and keeps working, with or without CodeFlow.

Do I need Claude?

Yes. CodeFlow opens the files that Claude makes with the free CodeFlow skill. For the best results use Claude Code or the Code tab of the Claude desktop app, because Claude can then preview and check its work on your computer. CodeFlow adds no AI cost of its own.

Can I convert a video I already made?

Yes. Videos made with HTML, SVG, CSS, GSAP or JavaScript work, including images and stock clips. Point Claude at the file and say make this for CodeFlow. You can also start a new video and get an MP4 preview and the editable file together.

Is the text really editable?

Yes: real text layers in the right font. One catch: if your SVGs come from Figma with Outline text turned on (its default), the words are only shapes and there's no text to recover. Export them again with Outline text unchecked.

Will it look exactly like my video?

In our tests, builds match the original at about 98–99% frame similarity. Things After Effects can't do natively, such as 3D or WebGL scenes and canvas particle effects, come in as pictures, and Claude tells you when that happens.

How many computers can I use it on?

Each licence works on 2 computers at the same time. If you change computers, free up a seat yourself from the Manage option in the panel.

Does it need internet?

Yes. CodeFlow checks your licence (or your free builds) online when it opens and before each build. Your projects and files never leave your computer: only your licence and computer details are checked.

How do I get updates?

When a new version is out, CodeFlow shows an update message in the panel. Download the newest installer from the top of this page and install it over the old one. Your licence keeps working.
