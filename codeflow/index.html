Questions

What's free and what costs money?

The extension and the Claude skill are both free to download, and you get 5 free builds on every computer. After that CodeFlow needs a lifetime license key: $20, paid once, for everyone. (Bangladesh has its own local price and shop.)

What happens after the 5 free builds?

CodeFlow asks for a license key before it builds more. Everything you've already built stays in your After Effects project and keeps working, with or without CodeFlow.

Do I need Claude?

Yes, to make the video. With the free CodeFlow skill, Claude writes your video as one HTML file. Any way of using Claude works (the app, the Code tab, Claude Code), because Claude only has to write a file. The conversion to After Effects is done by the CodeFlow panel itself, without AI, so CodeFlow adds no AI cost of its own.

Can I convert a video I already made?

Yes. Any HTML video that plays by itself (GSAP, CSS animations, SVG, canvas, plain JavaScript) can be dropped on the panel as it is: it finds the length and size itself. Video clips (H.264 MP4) come in as footage layers sized exactly as in your page; canvas drawing comes in as a picture sequence.

Is the text really editable?

Yes: real text layers in the right font. One catch: if your SVGs come from Figma with Outline text turned on (its default), the words are only shapes and there's no text to recover. Export them again with Outline text unchecked.

Will it look exactly like my video?

In our tests, built in After Effects, most videos match the original at 97–99% frame similarity, and the panel lists the parts that came in as pictures. Blend modes become blending modes and CSS 3D becomes an After Effects camera with 3D layers; canvas / WebGL drawing, gradients with 3+ colours and noise textures come in as pictures that still move with keyframes. Backdrop blur is not converted.

How many computers can I use it on?

Each license works on 2 computers at the same time. If you change computers, free up a seat yourself from the Manage option in the panel.

Does it need internet?

Yes. CodeFlow checks your license (or your free builds) online when it opens and before each build. Your projects and files never leave your computer: only your license and computer details are checked.

How do I get updates?

When a new version is out, CodeFlow shows an update message in the panel. Download the newest version from the top of this page and install it over the old one. Your license keeps working.
